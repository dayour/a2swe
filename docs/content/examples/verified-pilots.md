## Typical retained evidence

A retained pilot commonly includes:

- project research
- narration text
- generated timeline and subtitles
- resolved storyboard
- implemented `G1` shots
- versioned MP4
- overview and shot stills
- motion, frame, model, and media reports
- asset manifest
- delivery index
- updated companion ledger
