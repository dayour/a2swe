# CHAPTER 1 Connected farming
A field is not one uniform surface.
John Deere connects positioning,|machine displays, and field data|to help farmers work more precisely.
StarFire positioning helps align passes.
JDLink sends equipment data|to Operations Center,|where farmers can review performance|and plan the next job.
The point is not just a smarter tractor.
It is a connected operation,|guided by better information.
