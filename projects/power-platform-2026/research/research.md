## Qualifications

- “2026” identifies the review date and production edition, not universal feature availability.
- Release-plan entries do not prove GA, tenant rollout, entitlement, or licensing.
- Data policies reduce connector risk; they are not a complete security guarantee.
- Pipeline validation does not replace business-process, security, performance, or recovery testing.
- Solutions package metadata and components, not Dataverse table records.
- Product capabilities depend on environment, region, licensing, policy, and current documentation.
